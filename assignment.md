## Procedure

### 1.0 PURPOSE

Cybercriminals cater phishing attacks to university audiences through email with links or attachments that when opened, might install malware that can cause many problems ranging from locking you out of your device to stealing and sharing your data.
Phishing emails specifically trick users into revealing passwords, personal information, and other sensitive data. New NC State students may encounter phishing emails that imitate university offices, instructors, or other educational institutions.
